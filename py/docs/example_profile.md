The person here is invented. Every post, comment, search, artist and play was written or generated for `src/aas/example.py`, and no real person's data went in. They are a hospital pharmacist who works shifts, rides a bike, bakes bread and fixes old bicycles, and they brought 30 Reddit comments, 22 posts on X, 5 searches, 1 blog post(s) (1,598 words not counting searches), and 9,425 plays over 364 days.

The readers are the v1 defaults (`aas.example.default_readers`): text Big Five (bag of words trained on student essays), text MBTI (trained on a typology forum), the music reader, and the zero-data fused reader over the two text readers. The distilled text reader is not run, because it failed its pre-registered test (`distill.md`). Music is described on its own and never fused, since no link from listening to the Big Five has been tested.

## Reading it

Big Five values are z scores against the quiz norms, so 0 is average and about plus or minus 1 covers two people in three. Each interval claims to contain the person's quiz z 80% of the time. On this kind of writing the text readers state an accuracy close to chance (AUC 0.55 to 0.57 for this mix of writing, r about 0.11 to 0.16), so every Big Five interval is nearly as wide as the one that fits anybody (plus or minus 1.28), and every value sits close to 0. That is what v1 gives someone who mostly writes Reddit comments and posts on X. MBTI values are the chance of the first letter, pulled toward 50% to match an off-forum accuracy that is inferred and untested. The fused reader leaves `validity` empty because fusion has never been tested on real people with several sources. Its evidence gives the accuracy the model implies for the sources it combined, after the 0.75 discount.

Openness has no text Big Five number. The reader has only been shown to read openness from essay-like prose, and that is 22% of this person's words where at least half is needed. Openness still gets a fused value, from the MBTI N/S letter alone.
